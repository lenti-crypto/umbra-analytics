The business problem

02 / WHY IT MATTERED

Continuous market data, inconsistent reality

Market data arrived continuously from multiple exchanges, each with different formats, timing, symbols, and quality characteristics. A signal could look valuable while still being stale, duplicated, incomplete, or impossible to execute.

The analytical need

The team needed reliable reporting, anomaly detection, and a clear way to compare prices, spreads, and funding opportunities. The job was to make the data understandable enough for fast investigation without hiding uncertainty.

Evidence from the workflow

Four real product views showing the path from market overview to detailed investigation.

Case 01 / overview
Signal landscapeSpread · Splash · Funding

Dashboard overview — establish the shape of the data

The overview gives a fast read on event volume, the most active symbols, and the latest events across monitors. It is the starting point for checking whether an apparent market opportunity is part of a broad pattern or an isolated record.

What it showsEvent activity, top symbols, and the latest cross-monitor signals.
My contributionMetric validation, event grouping, time-window comparison, and anomaly triage.
Business valueCreated a clear path from an unusual event count to the symbol, venue, and time window that required investigation.
Case 02 / spread
Cross-exchange comparisonSpread

Spread dashboard — compare like with like

A structured table makes ticker, exchange route, price, spread, direction, and funding visible together. That layout supports quick validation of whether a spread is actionable or a symbol, route, or price-path issue.

What it showsReal-time spread comparison across exchanges.
My contributionSQL-based metric checks, route-level comparison, and missing-data review.
Business valueMade it easier to distinguish relevant opportunities from suspicious or non-routable price gaps.
Case 03 / funding
Rate consistencyFunding

Funding dashboard — surface deltas, not just rates

Funding opportunities become more useful when the rate delta, net effect, exchange pair, and settlement timing are visible in one row. This supports validation of stale timestamps and suspiciously large deviations.

What it showsFunding-rate deltas, exchange pairs, net spread, and settlement.
My contributionCross-source comparison, consistency checks, and reporting of outliers.
Business valueMade rate discrepancies easier to prioritize and explain.
Case 04 / time series
Temporal analysisSpread / Funding charts

Price and spread charts — investigate the event in time

Tables show what is true now; charts show how it became true. Comparing in/out prices, spread movement, and funding behavior over time helps separate persistent market structure from a short-lived data artifact.

What it showsPrice and spread movement over time for selected exchange routes.
My contributionTime-window selection, visual QA, and interpretation of discontinuities.
Business valueMade short-lived artifacts easier to distinguish from persistent market movements.
